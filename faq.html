Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Booking, delivery, deposits, setup and care: everything you need to know before you reserve.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and quinceañeras, 3–6 months ahead is ideal, especially for spring and fall Saturdays. Smaller events can often be booked a few weeks out. Ask us; we’ll tell you exactly what’s available.

Is there a minimum order?

Delivered orders have a minimum of $300 before delivery. There’s no minimum for will-call pickups.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is based on distance and on stairs or long carries. Within 15 miles it starts at $95 round trip. Your quote always shows it as its own line.

Do you set up and tear down?

Yes. Setup and teardown are available on every order and included in our packages. Our crew follows your floor plan and styles every table.

How much is the deposit?

A 50% deposit reserves your date and pieces. The balance is due 7 days before your event.

Can I change my counts after booking?

Of course. You can add or adjust items until 14 days before the event, subject to availability.

What if it rains?

For outdoor events we recommend reserving a tent. If weather turns, call us; we’ll work with you on the best option for your site.

Do I need to wash the linens or dishes?

No. Scrape food off plates and shake out the linens; we handle all the cleaning. Heavy wax or stains may carry a fee.

What happens if something breaks?

Accidents happen. Our optional damage waiver of 10% covers accidental breakage. Lost or stolen items are billed at replacement cost.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance for my venue?

Yes. We can issue a COI naming your venue; just send us the venue’s requirements at least 10 days ahead.

Can I pick up the rentals myself?

Many small items like linens, chargers and glassware can be picked up at our location by appointment. Tents, dance floors and draping are delivered and installed by our crew only.

What are your delivery and pickup times?

Most events are delivered the day before or the morning of, and picked up the next morning. Specific time windows and late-night pickups are available.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, Zelle, and cash.

What is your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable but can be applied to a new date within 12 months.

Still have a question?

Call, text or send us a note. We’re happy to talk through your event, even if you’re just starting to plan.
