What we do

Full service, start to finish.

Delivery & Pickup

Setup & Styling

Design Consultation

Tents & Outdoor Events

Your backyard, transformed.

Outdoor events take planning: ground conditions, access, power, lighting and a weather plan. We visit the site, recommend the right tent size for your guest count and layout, and install, level and anchor everything safely.
