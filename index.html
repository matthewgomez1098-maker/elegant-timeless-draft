Welcome to Elegant & Timeless

From the first chair to the last candle, every piece is chosen to be photographed.

We are a boutique event rental company built around one palette: gold, ivory and black, with soft blush and sage when the day calls for it. That focus means everything in our collection works together, so your tables look designed, not assembled.

Rent a single throne for a birthday portrait or dress a 250-guest tented reception. Either way, you get the same crew, the same care and one clear, itemized quote.

The E&T Standard

The details are the event.

Guests remember how a room felt. That feeling is built from small things done right: a linen that falls cleanly to the floor, a charger without a scratch, a glass with no water spots, a chair that doesn’t wobble.

  • Linens laundered and pressed before every event
  • Crystal and chargers hand-polished and inspected
  • Chairs and tables checked for level and finish
  • Set to your floor plan, down to the place card
